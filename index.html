Keyboard Hero

Gems fall down four lanes. Play a generated song, or load a track of your own and the game charts it for you. Hold the matching fret keys, then hit the strum bar as they cross the line. Chords need every fret shown. Gems with a tail are sustains: keep the frets held until the tail runs out for bonus points. Strumming with nothing under the line breaks your combo.
